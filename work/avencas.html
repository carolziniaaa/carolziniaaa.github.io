Avencas

Avencas is an outdoor clothing brand built around coastal and marine environments, guided by circular economy principles and a genuine commitment to sustainable living. Its roots are in Avencas beach, Cascais, part of a legally protected marine area where biodiversity and humanity try to coexist without harm.

The place itself shaped the brand, a sacred, living ecosystem where everything is connected. That openness and respect for all beings is what the clothes are made for, and made from.

Raw materials, multiple lives, nothing wasted. A clothing brand that, like the beach it comes from, takes care of what it's given. For those who explore with care, create with purpose, and wear their values on their skin.
